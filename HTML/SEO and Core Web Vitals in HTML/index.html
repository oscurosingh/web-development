About Lord Krishna

Lord Krishna is a major deity in Hinduism, revered as the eighth avatar of the god Vishnu. He is known for his role in the epic Mahabharata, particularly the Bhagavad Gita, where he serves as a charioteer and guide to Prince Arjuna. Krishna is celebrated for his divine playfulness, wisdom, and teachings on dharma (duty/righteousness). His life stories include his childhood exploits in Vrindavan, his role in the Kurukshetra War, and his teachings on love and devotion.
